# Career Reality Check & Next Move (India + AI era)
*Appended: Aug 2026 — blunt advice based on this story, Amex SRE work, and LearnJava path*

## Brutal honesty first

1. **Your degrees will not carry interviews in India.** 6.6 LPU + theory-heavy MS is fine as a checkbox. Nobody in a GCC / product hiring loop will care once they see 3+ years Amex. They will care if you can code under pressure and tell crisp production stories.

2. **“I can understand any Java project” ≠ “I can clear a Java developer bar.”** Reading production code and finding bugs is real skill. Writing clean Java, data structures, concurrency, system design, and owning features under a 45–60 min interview is a different sport. Treat the gap as real until your LearnJava + DSA work proves otherwise.

3. **Do not quit SRE to become a junior Java fresher narrative.** That is a downgrade story in India’s market. Pure “Java developer – 3 years experience” without shipped features on your resume gets compared to people who only wrote CRUD for 3 years and grinded LeetCode. You lose the uniqueness of Amex-scale production.

4. **Do not chase “AI Engineer / GenAI” as a rebrand.** India hiring is hot for AI/ML/GenAI titles, but those loops expect Python, ML basics, RAG/LLM app building, or model ops — not “I used ChatGPT.” Fake AI on the resume dies in the first screen. Use AI as a **multiplier on your real stack**, not a new identity overnight.

5. **India IT in 2026 is not 2018.** Mass hiring for generalists is weak. Demand skews to **specialized mid/senior skills**: cloud, platform/SRE/DevOps, data, cybersecurity, AI-adjacent. GCCs (captives of US/EU companies) pay better and want ownership; service companies (TCS/Infosys-style) still hire but compress growth for “ops-only” profiles. Aim **GCC / product / strong mid-size product**, not “any IT job.”

6. **Comfort zone is real.** Leading calls, finding issues others miss, Ansible/kubectl toil reduction — that is valuable. It can also become a golden cage: respected in ops, never forced to build. Leaving comfort means **shipping code + measurable platform outcomes**, not only being the smartest person on the bridge call.

## Resume tips / tricks (India + your profile)

### Positioning line (example)
> Site Reliability / Platform Engineer — Java & Kubernetes | Operated large-scale event platforms; improved observability, reduced toil with automation, led cross-team production incidents.

### Do
- **Numbers:** events/day order of magnitude, MTTF/MTTR improvement, #clusters, #playbooks, time saved, incident severity.
- **Verbs of ownership:** identified, fixed root cause, designed, automated, proposed to eng teams, led bridge call.
- **Tech grouped:** Languages | Data/Messaging | Orchestration | Observability | Cloud/OS.
- **Projects section:** 2 personal projects with GitHub links (Java service + AI-ops or K8s automation).

- Hide Amex scale; also don’t claim “built the whole platform” if you didn’t — interviewers will probe.
- Put GenAI as job title without artifacts.
- Lead with CGPA or “copied in exams” energy on any public material — that honesty is for *you*; resume shows competence now.
